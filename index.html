Selected project

My private NanoClaw

I maintain a private fork of NanoClaw as a personal assistant that I use day to day. I adapt it around my own routines. I use it to keep and revisit notes from conversations, turn books and articles into structured courses, save vocabulary while I read for later review, and return interactive components such as maps when a text reply is not enough.

The repository remains private because its history includes personal configuration and data. Any examples published here will come from real runs with the personal details removed.
